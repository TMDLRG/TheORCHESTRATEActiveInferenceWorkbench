2 · Assemble it

Energy minus looseness

Part one — energy. For each possible cause, ask: under my belief, how unexpected is “this cause together with the sky I saw”? That togetherness is the joint from Module 2, p(y, η) = p(y|η)·p(η) — likelihood (fit) times prior (stretch), already fused. Its surprise is −ln p(y, η). Weight by how much your belief backs each cause, and add up. That’s the energy — low when your belief backs causes that sit comfortably with what you saw.

Part two — looseness. Subtract your belief’s spread — its willingness to stay uncommitted (its entropy). The more honestly loose, the more we subtract, the lower the height.

Put them together — energy minus looseness — and you have the height:

F[q] = Ση q(η) · [ ln q(η) − ln p(y, η) ]

For each cause: take how confident your belief is there (ln q) minus how well that cause sits with the sky (ln p(y,η)), weight by your belief q(η), and sum. That one number is free energy — the height under a belief.

▸ Go deeper — three faces of one number derivation
F[q] = E_q[ln q(η) − ln p(y,η)] = E_q[−ln p(y,η)] − H[q] — energy minus entropy. It rearranges to complexity − accuracy: F = KL(q(η)‖p(η)) − E_q[ln p(y|η)], the stretch-from-prior term minus the fit term, with looseness folded in. Same number, three faces. This exact two-state sum carries the bound tag :exact.
3 · Look what isn’t there · 4 · Land a real number

The evidence never enters — and it still computes

Now the moment this climb was for. Stare at the formula and hunt for the evidence — p(y), the sum over all hidden causes that walled you off in Module 2. Commit a yes/no in your head, then press the button.

The computable height

See F assembled from the joint terms only, hunt for the evidence (it isn’t there), then compute F by hand — twice. The reveal is that F needs only the joint, never the forbidden sum — not a plotted curve.

The formula, colour-coded
F[q] = Ση q(η) · [ ln q(η) − ln p(y, η) ]
uses your belief q and the joint p(y,η)=p(y|η)·p(η) — one multiply per cause.
It’s not there. p(y) = Σ p(y,η), the forbidden total, appears nowhere in F. The height uses only your belief q (you chose it) and the joint p(y,η) (likelihood × prior — one multiply per cause, no sum). So free energy is computable — fully, exactly — even though the surprise it shadows is not. That is the whole trick: a score for your beliefs that never walks through the wall.
