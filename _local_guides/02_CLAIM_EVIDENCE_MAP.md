**Evidence-strength legend**

- **5 — Strong repo support.** Verbatim or near-verbatim in book extracts; or in `equations.ex` with `:verified_against_source[_and_appendix]`; or in the canonical glossary.
- **4 — Partial repo support.** Repo establishes a closely related framing; the exact phrasing is mine.
- **3 — General background.** Standard in the field; the repo is consistent but not the originating source.
- **2 — Interpretive synthesis.** My way of stitching repo material together. Plausible but not the only reading.
- **1 — Speculative.** Suggestive of where AIF *might* extend; not established by the repo.
